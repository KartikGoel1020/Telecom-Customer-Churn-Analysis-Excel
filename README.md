# 📊 Telecommunications Customer Churn Analysis Using Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is a major challenge for telecommunications companies because losing customers can affect revenue, customer retention, and market share.

This project analyzes telecommunications customer data to explore patterns associated with customer churn. The analysis is based on a case study in which Airtel aims to understand why customers discontinue their services and identify opportunities to improve retention.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the proportion of churned and non-churned customers.
- Investigate relationships between customer tenure and churn.
- Examine the impact of international plans and voicemail subscriptions.
- Analyze customer service calls and their relationship with churn.
- Explore calling patterns, usage, and associated charges.
- Identify customer segments that may require targeted retention strategies.
- Develop actionable recommendations based on the analysis.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- Data Analysis
- Descriptive Statistics
- Data Segmentation
- Charts and Data Visualization

## 📈 Analysis Performed

### 1. Basic Analysis
- Churned versus non-churned customer distribution.
- Customer account length and churn.
- International plan subscription and churn.
- Customer service calls and churn.
- Day, evening, and night calling charges.
- Voicemail plan subscription and churn.
- International calling usage and charges.

### 2. Intermediate Analysis
- Churn segmentation by area code.
- Customer service call thresholds and churn.
- Customer tenure segmentation.
- Evening calling patterns and churn.
- Voice mailbox usage and customer retention.
- Relationships between international calling behavior and churn.
- Customer usage patterns and segmentation.

### 3. Advanced Analysis
- Predictive analysis concepts for customer churn.
- Customer segmentation based on service usage.
- Interaction between customer attributes and churn.
- Potential revenue impact of customer attrition.

*The analyses listed above describe the project's scope. Refer to the Excel workbook to verify which questions have been fully analyzed and answered.*

## 💡 Business Value

The analysis can help telecommunications businesses:

- Identify customer groups with elevated churn rates.
- Investigate potential dissatisfaction associated with customer service interactions.
- Evaluate whether subscription features align with customer needs.
- Explore opportunities for targeted retention campaigns.
- Use customer behavior insights to support data-driven decisions.
